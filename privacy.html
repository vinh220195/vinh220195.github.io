Privacy Policy

The app sends XAUUSD trading signals to your phone over WebSocket and push notification. This document explains what we collect, why, and what you control.

Trading Signals ("the app", "we") is built and run by an independent developer, not a financial firm or brokerage. The app forwards trade signals produced by an automated trading strategy and never places a trade on your behalf — you always make the final call.

01

Data we collect

Three categories, nothing more:

Automatic

Device identifiers

A random device ID and a device token issued by our server specifically for your phone on first install, plus a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for push delivery. Not your phone number, not an IMEI, not your contacts or GPS location.

You provide it

Email and MT5 account number (optional)

Only if you submit a verification request on the Referral tab to unlock full price targets — used to manually cross-check your account against the broker's partner system, for no other purpose.

Automatic

App usage data

Firebase Analytics records general usage metrics (app opens, which screens get viewed, device/OS version, crash reports) so we know what's working. Not tied to your name or email.

02

What we use this data for

  • Delivering signals to your specific device — over the open WebSocket connection, and via push notification when the app is backgrounded.
  • Telling devices apart and protecting each one individually — every device holds its own credential, so if one is compromised or abused we can lock out that exact device without touching anyone else.
  • Verifying and managing your unlock tier — cross-checking the MT5 account you provide against the broker's partner program, and tracking which unlock code is bound to which device.
  • Improving the app — understanding which features get used, catching bugs through aggregated Analytics data.
03

Sharing with third parties

We never sell your data to anyone, in any form.

Two parties can see part of it:

  • Google / Firebase — runs the push notification (FCM) and usage-analytics (Analytics) infrastructure. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • The broker's partner program — the email and MT5 account number you submit (if any) are only cross-checked by hand in the partner dashboard to confirm eligibility, never sent through any automated API.
04

Storage & security

Data lives on a server we operate directly, with no intermediary in between. Some specific protections:

  • Device credentials are stored server-side only as a hash — the server itself can't read the original value back out.
  • The credential kept on your phone lives in Android's encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences), never as plain text.
  • Every connection between the app and the server runs over HTTPS/WSS.

Data is retained for as long as the device/account stays active, or until you request deletion (see Your rights below).

05

Your rights

You can ask us to view, correct, or delete all data tied to your device at any time — reach out using the contact details at the bottom of this page. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete server-side data; message us if you want it removed entirely.

06

Age requirement

The app deals with financial trading, which carries risk, and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

07

Financial disclaimer

Signals in this app are for informational purposes only — not investment advice or a personalized financial recommendation. Forex/gold trading carries a risk of loss. Every trading decision is yours alone.
